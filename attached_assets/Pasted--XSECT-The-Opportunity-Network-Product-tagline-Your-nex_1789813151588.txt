# XSECT

## The Opportunity Network

### Product tagline

**Your next opportunity is already on your path.**

### Core concept

People cross paths every day.

Most professional opportunities disappear with those crossings.

**XSECT** discovers meaningful intersections between:

* People
* Professional intent
* Skills
* Opportunities
* Location
* Time
* Availability
* Events
* Existing relationships
* Trust
* Organizations
* Professional goals

XSECT helps users discover people and opportunities they might otherwise never encounter while keeping identity, contact information, and exact location protected until mutual consent.

---

# 1. PRODUCT VISION

Build XSECT as a premium, venture-scale, mobile-first professional opportunity network.

Do NOT position it as:

* a dating app
* a generic social network
* a LinkedIn clone
* a job board
* a freelancer marketplace
* a “people nearby” app
* a generic AI networking assistant

XSECT is:

> **A professional opportunity intelligence network that detects meaningful intersections between people, intent, time, place, and professional networks.**

The fundamental object in the system is an:

# XSECT

An XSECT occurs when two or more relevant professional signals intersect.

Examples:

### Physical XSECT

Two professionals physically cross paths.

### Intent XSECT

One person's Want matches another person's Offer.

### Network XSECT

Two people are connected through an intermediate professional.

### Time XSECT

Two compatible professional needs become relevant at the same time.

### Event XSECT

People with complementary professional intent attend the same event.

### Opportunity XSECT

A person's goal intersects with an available opportunity.

The platform should make these intersections discoverable.

# 12. XSECT MATCHING ENGINE

Create a real matching engine.

Do not randomly generate compatibility.

Calculate XSECT Score using:

```text
Intent compatibility
+
Skill compatibility
+
Industry compatibility
+
Goal compatibility
+
Geographic proximity
+
Availability overlap
+
Urgency
+
Trust compatibility
+
Recent activity
+
Opportunity freshness
+
Network proximity
```

Normalize to 0–100.

Store the individual scoring factors.

Example:

```text
XSECT SCORE
92

Intent       98
Skills       94
Timing       91
Location     88
Trust        95
Freshness    93
```

Always explain the recommendation.
